// The Problem

Three things are quietly killing Indian space startups.

Talk to enough founders building hardware in this country and the same three walls show up, in the same order, every time.

01

No testing access

Environmental, vibration and thermal test facilities are scarce and booked out for early-stage teams. Many end up shipping hardware abroad just to get it qualified.

02

No patient capital

Hardware timelines run in years, not quarters. Most capital in India still expects software-speed returns, so funding often dries up right when a program needs it most.

03

No first customer

Nobody wants to fly an unproven component first, and without a flight record, nobody gets the chance to prove it. The loop rarely breaks on its own.

GalOort starts with the part of this loop we can move fastest on: how space companies and suppliers actually find each other.

We're building the infrastructure India's private space companies can thrive in.
